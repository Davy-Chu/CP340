## Proposed promotional strategy

**Target audience:** Students, remote workers, and people improving their home
workspace, especially those working with limited desk space or a small budget.

**Brand message:** Small, purposeful improvements can make an everyday workspace
feel calmer, more organized, and ready for focused work.

**Instagram:** Three posts per week featuring desk setups, product photos, and
workspace organization tips. Use practical captions and invite readers to explore
the relevant WorkNest product or blog guide.

**TikTok:** Two to three short videos per week featuring desk transformations,
productivity tips, and product demonstrations. Show a specific problem, a simple
change, and the resulting desk setup.

**Tone:** Calm, useful, approachable, and minimalist.

**Purpose and measurement:** Build awareness and direct interested viewers to
the WorkNest website. Review saves, shares, profile visits, and website-link
clicks in the platforms' available insights to identify useful topics. This is
a proposed schedule; no social posts or accounts have been published by this change.
